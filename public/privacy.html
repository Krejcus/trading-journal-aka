Alpha Trade Privacy Policy

Effective: August 11, 2026

Alpha Trade is a trading journal, analytics, risk-management and account-connectivity application operated by Filip Krejča. This policy explains how information is handled when you use Alpha Trade and connect a Tradovate account.

Information we process

  • Account and profile details needed to authenticate and operate the application.
  • Trading-account data authorized by you, such as accounts, orders, fills, positions and risk values.
  • Journal entries, trading plans, reviews, preferences and application diagnostics.
  • OAuth authorization data needed to maintain a connection. Alpha Trade does not request or store your Tradovate password.

How information is used

Information is used only to provide the journal, analytics, reconciliation, risk-management and user-requested account-connectivity features; to secure and maintain the service; and to comply with applicable legal obligations. Alpha Trade does not sell personal information.

Tradovate and market data

Tradovate connectivity is initiated and authorized by the user. Alpha Trade does not redistribute Tradovate or exchange market data. Third-party services, including Tradovate and hosting providers, process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

Security and retention

Access is limited to the permissions necessary for enabled features. OAuth credentials and tokens are handled server-side and must not be exposed in browser code or public repositories. Information is retained only as long as needed for the service, security, legal obligations or user-requested history.

Your choices

You may revoke Tradovate authorization, disconnect an account, or request access, correction or deletion of personal information, subject to legal and security retention requirements.
